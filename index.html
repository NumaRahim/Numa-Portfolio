✦ About Me

Here’s who I am & what I do

My work sits at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Software Engineering, and No-Code Automation. From building LLM-powered research agents and computer-vision applications to designing automated business workflows, I bring both the technical and practical sides of AI together.

I’ve worked with LLMs, LangChain, RAG, Gemini, TensorFlow, Keras, Scikit-learn, OpenCV, MediaPipe, and TensorFlow Lite, while also building scalable automation workflows with GoHighLevel, Make, n8n, Zapier, and Unbounce.

One of my key projects, Signora, is an AI-powered real-time sign-language recognition and translation application. I worked across the ML pipeline — from data preprocessing and model training to evaluation and TensorFlow Lite deployment.

I’ve also built an AI Research Agent using Gemini and LangChain that performs multi-step research, retrieves information from multiple sources, and generates structured, source-grounded responses.

What drives me

I’m fascinated by what happens when AI meets automation. Whether it’s an AI agent performing research, a machine-learning model understanding visual data, or an automated workflow connecting multiple business systems — I love turning complex processes into smart, scalable, and efficient systems.
