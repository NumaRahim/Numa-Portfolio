Young Innovators

Childcare Lead Generation & Automation System

Introduction

Young Innovators is a childcare education and coaching business that provides courses designed to support childcare professionals and individuals in developing the knowledge and skills needed for effective child upbringing and childcare business management. For this project, I developed a complete automated course sales and lead-generation system for Young Innovators. The system included the design and implementation of multiple conversion funnels, lead capture workflows, automated follow-ups, and course enrollment processes. I also integrated Stripe for secure course payments and implemented coupon generation to support promotional campaigns. Once a customer purchases a course, the automation system handles the post-purchase journey automatically. Customers receive confirmation emails through Gmail, course/access links, scheduled reminders, and relevant follow-up communications, reducing manual work while keeping customers engaged throughout the course journey.
